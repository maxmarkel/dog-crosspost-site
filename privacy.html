Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Dog Crosspost is a personal tool used only by its owner to copy videos from the owner's own Instagram account to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

  • Instagram: the owner's own posts (video files, captions, post dates and links), read through the Instagram Graph API.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel.

How the data is used and stored

Video files are downloaded only long enough to upload them to YouTube and are deleted right after. The tool keeps a small record of which Instagram posts have been uploaded and their YouTube video IDs, so nothing is uploaded twice. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is sent only to Instagram and YouTube as described above.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (YouTube) and in Facebook's Business Integrations settings (Instagram).
